# TORCH Token — Holder Rewards

## Overview

TORCH is the community token for torch.market. Holders get paid, forever.

20% of all dev fees earned on torch.market are used to buy TORCH on the open market and airdrop to holders. This has no end date. As long as torch.market generates fees, holders earn.

The team currently holds 200M TORCH (20% of supply). These tokens will be distributed to holders as part of early airdrops. Once the reserve is distributed, buybacks continue with protocol revenue alone.

## Who Qualifies

Any wallet holding **1M+ TORCH** at snapshot time.

## The Loop

1. torch.market earns protocol fees
2. 20% of dev fees buy TORCH on the open market
3. Monthly snapshot of qualifying holders
4. Airdrop pro-rata based on holdings
5. Repeat forever

No staking. No locking. No claim portals. Tokens show up in your wallet.

## Why

The token is the community. The protocol serves the community. Revenue flows back to the people who believed in it.
